Why We Created Bali Elopement

"True love does not need a ballroom of 300 spectators to feel real. Sometimes, the most sacred vows are whispered with only the sound of waves crashing against ancient cliffs."

For over a decade, our parent company, Bali Top Wedding, has orchestrated legendary luxury weddings across the island. Yet as the world evolved, we observed more and more couples—particularly from Europe, the UK, the Americas, and Australia—seeking an alternative to the exhausting pressure and high costs of traditional weddings.

They wanted something deeply personal. A day where every second belongs to them. A wedding that feels like a soulful vacation, not a stressful performance.

That is why Bali Elopement was born: a dedicated boutique division combining the unmatched operational power, legal network, and luxury vendor relationships of Bali Top Wedding with an intimate, clean, editorial sensibility.
