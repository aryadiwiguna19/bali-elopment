# Bali Elopement Service

An intimate luxury celebration boutique dedicated exclusively to elopement weddings, pre-wedding journeys, and private villa gatherings in Bali.
Powered & Organized by [Bali Top Wedding](https://www.balitopwedding.com).

## Signature Offerings

- **Intimate Elopement Wedding**: Just the two of you (or up to 6 close guests) exchanging eternal vows overlooking dramatic azure cliffs or secluded shores.
- **Pre-Wedding Adventure**: Full-day cinematic photo & video journey across Bali's natural wonders.
- **Private Villa Wedding**: Luxury private villa ceremony and candlelit dinner with up to 30 cherished guests.

## Features

- **Modern Luxury Aesthetic**: Clean Coastal Slate (`#5A7A85`) & Warm Ivory (`#FAF6F0`) palette.
- **Dynamic Modular Architecture**: Header and Footer components loaded cleanly with centralized configuration.
- **Interactive Vertical Cinema**: Built-in 9:16 vertical wedding films for real couple showcases.
- **Direct Multi-Channel Inquiry**: Streamlined WhatsApp fast-booking and direct email correspondence.
